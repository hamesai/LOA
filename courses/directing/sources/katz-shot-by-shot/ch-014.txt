ONLINE RESOURCES
Many principles of ﬁlmmaking and design are the same in both the analog
and digital realms. This is not true of computer software, where the
majority of ﬁlm design and production take place. This includes shooting
on location if you are using a digital camera that is a species of computer
too. Things change yearly/monthly, so http://shotbyshotbook.com will
provide more up-to-date information on digital resources available online.
There’s a lot of it, but the quality varies.
Here are some of the best and hopefully stable sources of information and
learning for the nitty-gritty of digital production and software.

Lynda.com
In-depth training in popular software such as Adobe: After Effects,
Photoshop, Premiere, Cinema 4D, Maya, and dozens of other mediarelated software applications.
Redgiant.com
This is a visual effects software site with great tutorials. They have
positioned themselves to help indie ﬁlmmakers. In addition to VFX
products such as Trapcode, they have many tools for color correction of
indie movies. The folks that run the company are ﬁlmmakers.
Pluralsight.com (formerly Digital Tutors)
Visual effects and game production software training at a high level for
software such as Maya, Cinema 4D, Houdini, ZBrush, Blender,
Photoshop, and many more.
Fxphd.com
Technical training in several key VFX software applications as well as
art/tech theory for digital production.
Cinemetrics.lv
A fascinating scientiﬁc/statistical approach to ﬁlm study by measuring
the length of shots, the number of shots, and other quantitative
measurements over the history of ﬁlm. An academic site by ﬁlm scholar
Barry Salt.
