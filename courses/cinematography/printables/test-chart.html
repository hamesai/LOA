Test chart: exposure, focus and framing

Print at 100% (no scaling) on plain white paper, tape it to a flat wall, and light it evenly. Frame so the red outer box just fills your frame. The grey steps are printed greys, so use a proper 18% grey card for metering and use this chart to compare steps, not to calibrate. Use it for the Module 3 lens test and the Module 6 grey-card bracket.
