More importantly, though, we hope you keep finding new answers to these two key questions: 
• What story must you tell? 
• What emotion must you share? 
Asking these questions will provide you with endless possibilities and new opportunities to learn. They 
will give you deep insight and always challenge you. They will keep you humble, curious, and open. 
Build a broad base of reference and, with it, the ability to connect with people from all walks of life. 
Make films that will force the audience to think, question, feel, and connect. 
Make a bunch of bad films so you can make one that is truly worthy of their time, a film tha t allows 
people to see beyond their perception and share in the delight of a collective experience. 
Work hard to earn the trust of your audience. 
Work hard to earn the trust of your colleagues. 
Do your best, and be generous with your time. Every project you work on will make you better, add 
to your humanity, and increase your wisdom. 
Work with people who are more skilled than you. Lift those who are still learning. 
Embrace being both a student and a teacher. 
Continue the conversation. 
Enjoy the ride.
