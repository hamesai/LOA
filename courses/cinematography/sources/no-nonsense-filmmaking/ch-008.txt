This happens in different stages: 
1. Sound editorial:Sound editorial: Having received the locked picture from the editor, the sound work begins 
by cleaning up the dialogue tracks to smooth out the levels and remove unwanted sounds. 
Dialogue may need to be rerecorded if the production tracks are unusable. 
2. Sound designSound design: Effects are added from sound effects libraries or created 
specifically for the film. This includes Foley effects. 
3. Rerecording and final mixRerecording and final mix: All the elements are brought together to create 
the final soundtrack. The final mix is often done in a room that mimics a 
movie theatre, thereby ensuring that the film will sound correct when it is 
screened by an audience. 
Multiple versions of the final mix may be created for different modes of distribution. A theatrical mix 
is quite different from that required to stream online. Be sure you have the right mix for your screening 
needs.
