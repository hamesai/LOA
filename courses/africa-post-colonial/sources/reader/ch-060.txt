Climatic change in Africa. The chart shows the extent to which climatic conditions in the Sahara have been more humid and more arid than at present over the last 22,000 years. It is to be expected that conditions elsewhere in Africa have been similarly variable. The width of the curve reflects divergences between regions, contradictory evidence or variations in dating. (After Muzzolini, 1993, p.228)
