**Not read**

- No specific video was verified.

**Where sources differ (shown side by side in the lessons)**
- Fridge limit: UK under 8 C in a power cut (FSA), Ireland 0 to 5 C (FSAI), US 40 F (FSIS).
- Milk, yoghurt and eggs after the fridge fails: FSIS discards perishables above 40 F for over 2 hours; the UK CMO script for a long national outage allows some for days if they look and smell normal. The course says to use the stricter rule when other safe food exists, and always for at-risk people. That recommendation is our reading.
- Refreezing: FSAI says do not refreeze defrosted food unless first cooked above 75 C; FSIS and CDC allow refreezing while ice crystals remain.

**Our inference or framing (labelled in the lessons)**
- Scaling arithmetic (for example bleach per litre from the CDC per-gallon ratio), the "stricter rule" advice, practical tips such as filling bottles before a warning, and the 72-hour skeleton table.

**Illustrative numbers**
All households, dates and times used in examples are invented.
