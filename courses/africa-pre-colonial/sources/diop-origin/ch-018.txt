Chapter II

1. The oldest Egyptian monuments which depict all the races of the earth — the bas-reliefs of Biban-el-Moluk, for example — show that during those early epochs only the so-called Nordic race was tattooed.

2. The yellow race as well was probably the result of crossbreeding between Blacks and Whites at a very ancient time in the history of mankind. The ethnic features of yellow peoples — lips, nose, prognathism — are those of the mixed breed. Their facial features (high cheekbones, puffed eyelids, Mongolian pucker, slant eyes, depression at the bridge of the nose) could merely result from the effect of thousands of years in a climate of cold winds on the face.
