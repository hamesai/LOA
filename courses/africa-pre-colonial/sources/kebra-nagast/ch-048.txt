ii6. Concerning the Chariot of Ethiopia 

And the Archbishops answered and said unto Gre¬ 
gory, the Worker of Wonders, “ Behold now, thou hast 
told us concerning the vanquisher of the enemy of 
Rome, and now [tell us] of the chariot of Ethiopia and 
whether it shall remain henceforward, to the Coming of 
Christ, as thou hast told us concerning Zion, and con¬ 
cerning the faith of the people of Ethiopia, and likewise 
if their chariot shall remain.” And Gregory said unto 
them, “ It shall assuredly not disappear. And again, 
hearken ye unto me and I will declare this unto you : 
A few Jews shall lift up their heads against our faith in 
Nagran and in Armenia in the days after this, and this 
God will do by His Will so that He may destroy them, 
for Armenia is a territory of Rome and Nagran is a 
territory of Ethiopia.”
