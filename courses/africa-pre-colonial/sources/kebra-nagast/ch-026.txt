77. Concerning the King of Persia 

And the King of Persia is likewise of the seed of Shem, 
and we will inform you concerning the matters that 
relate to him. Judah begot two sons, and he brought 
in Te‘mar (Tamar) for his eldest son, and he died. 
And Judah sent his younger son to her that he might 
raise up seed to his brother by his brother’s wife. And 
he did that which God hated, and he did not wish to 
raise up seed to his brother as his father Judah had 
commanded him. Now when he lay with Tamar he 
made his seed to go into the ground, so that it might 
not germinate in her womb and be called the seed of 
his brother, but he wished to raise up seed by his own 
wife in his own name. And when God saw his evil act 
He turned His face away from him and slew him. And 
Judah, the father-in-law of Tamar, brought her back, 
and set her in the house of her father, and said unto her
