23. How the Merchant returned to Ethiopia 

And it came to pass that the merchant Tarnrin 
wished to return to his own country, and he went to 
Solomon and bowed low before him, and embraced 
him, and said unto him, “ Peace be to thy majesty ! 
Send me away and let me depart to my country to 
my Lady, for I have tarried long in beholding thy 
glory, and thy wisdom, and the abundance of dainty 
meats wherewith thou hast regaled me. And now 
I would depart to my Lady. Would that I could abide 
with thee, even as one of the very least of thy servants, 
for blessed are they who hear thy voice and perform 
thy commands ! Would that I could abide here and 
never leave thee ! but thou must send me away to my 
Lady because of what hath been committed to my 
charge, so that I may give unto her her property. 
And as for myself, I am her servant.” And Solomon 
went into his house and gave unto him whatever 
valuable thing he desired for the country of Ethiopia, 
and he sent him away in peace. And Tarnrin bade 
him farewell, and went forth, and journeyed along his 
road, and came to his Lady, and delivered over to her 
all the possessions which he had brought. And he re¬
