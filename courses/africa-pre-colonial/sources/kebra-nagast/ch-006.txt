15. Concerning Isaac and Jacob 

And Isaac his son became king, and he did not trans¬ 
gress the commandment of God ; and he was pure in 
his soul and in his body, and he died in honour. And 
his son Jacob reigned, and he also did not transgress the 
commandment of God, and his possessions became nu¬ 
merous, and his children were many ; and God blessed 
him and he died in honour.
